features:
  - title: For keeping mail, not sending it
    details: Messages are stored as ordinary .eml files. You can't send or reply, and your mailbox is never changed.
  - title: Stays on your computer
    details: One program that only your own computer can connect to. All your data is in a single folder.
  - title: Search that handles Korean
    details: Search sender, subject, body and attachment names at once, with filters like `from:` and `has:attachment`. Korean initial-consonant search (초성검색) works too.
  - title: New mail as it arrives
    details: Turn on background sync for an IMAP profile and new mail shows up within seconds on servers that support it, with an optional desktop notification.
  - title: Every attachment in one place
    details: Browse all attachments across your mail by type, sender or date, and see what takes up space on the stats page.
  - title: Backups and exports
    details: Copy new mail into a backup folder every day or week, or export just a search's results as .eml files, an .mbox or a PDF.
  - title: Opens Korean and Office documents
    details: Preview Hangul (HWP, HWPX), Word and Excel attachments without leaving the app or installing anything.
  - title: Works with any S3 storage
    details: Import from and export to AWS S3, or S3-compatible storage such as MinIO, Cloudflare R2 and Backblaze B2.
